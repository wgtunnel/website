## Origin story

I started WG Tunnel in 2023 as an open-source hobby project with one simple goal: build an on-demand
VPN app for WireGuard on Android. The official app didn't have it, and I was tired of manually
toggling my VPN every time I got home, or tinkering with unreliable Tasker scripts to do it for me.

As it turned out, I wasn't the only one. The app quickly gained traction, and with it came requests
for other features the official app was missing: deeper Android integration, proxy support,
AmneziaWG, and more. Before long, I was fielding and building a plethora of features I'd never
planned for, and the project goal had shifted from a simple on-demand VPN app to **building the best
WireGuard client on Android**.

Then the requests for a desktop client started rolling in. After a lot of research and planning, I
spent an entire winter and into the spring building the first version of WG Tunnel for desktop. With
that, the project goal shifted once more: **to build the best WireGuard client, period**.

## The community

I'm endlessly grateful for the community that has grown around WG Tunnel. You help drive the
direction of the app and keep the project alive through translations, testing, feedback, and
small-dollar donations. This project would not be possible without you. Thank you!

## My promise

Every part of WG Tunnel, Android and desktop, is fully open source. No trackers, no closed-source
dependencies, no paywalled features, and it's going to stay that way. I've received multiple offers
from various... I'll be generous and call them "acquisition firms" looking to buy things like the
app and its Google Play listing. That will never happen. **WG Tunnel will remain independently
funded and community driven.**

## Keeping WG Tunnel going

WG Tunnel has nearly a quarter million users, and I build and maintain it alongside part-time work,
as a father, a husband, and the sole provider for my family. Most nights, you'll find me responding
to messages, helping users debug issues, or developing new features.

Since 2023, I've shipped over 130 releases across Android and desktop. The costs are real: test
devices, development tools, and the code-signing certificates that Apple and Windows require. These
are all paid out of my own pocket.

I'm not asking for a handout. Donations decide how much of my time can go to WG Tunnel instead of
other work or time with my family. My dream is to work on WG Tunnel full time, and your support is
what could make that possible. Supporting the app means more features, faster fixes, and quicker
help. If you've found WG Tunnel to be of value, please consider [supporting the project](/donate).
